## Parties

This template contemplates a **mutual** NDA between `[DISCLOSING/RECEIVING PARTY A]` and
`[DISCLOSING/RECEIVING PARTY B]`. No party is named or bound by this draft.

## 1. Purpose

To permit a prospective evaluator to review a **source-free, redacted** diligence bundle describing
a research-stage ETH/USD research program, solely to assess a *potential* future relationship. The
evaluation conveys no license and no source.

## 2. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means the redacted diligence bundle (evaluation contract, claims
catalogue, factsheet, readiness scorecard) and any discussions about it, marked or reasonably
understood as confidential. It **excludes** anything the disclosing party never provides — source
code, sealed research partitions, raw or canonical price data, credentials, and live/paper access.

## 3. Obligations of the Receiving Party

- Use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose in §1.
- Not disclose it to third parties without prior written consent.
- Protect it with at least reasonable care.
- Not attempt to reconstruct withheld materials (source, sealed data) from the redacted bundle.

## 4. Exclusions

Standard exclusions apply: information that is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving
party; independently developed; rightfully received from a third party; or required to be disclosed
by law (with notice where permitted).

## 5. No License, No Warranty, No Offer

- No license or IP right is granted by this agreement or by the evaluation.
- The bundle is provided "as is"; research-train results are **not** out-of-sample, forward, or live
  performance and are not warranted.
- Nothing in this agreement or the evaluation is an offer to sell or a commitment to transact.

## 6. Term and Return

The confidentiality obligations survive for `[N]` years. On request, the receiving party destroys or
returns Confidential Information.
