# V2 Prospective Next-Phase Plan

## 16. Prospective next-phase governance (documentation only)

This section is governance documentation. It activates nothing, authorizes nothing, and
computes nothing. It records the conditions any future strategy-research phase must satisfy
after the V2A-V2B stacked acceptance, and the data-only prospective program that may later be
proposed under a separate human authorization. No code here is wired to run, no partition is
opened, and no candidate is defined. Nothing here changes the standing posture: V2 remains not
sell-ready.

### 16.1 The stacked result this plan descends from

V2A evaluated three pre-registered ETH-only candidate families and nominated none. V2B added a
genuinely-new BTC information source, evaluated two pre-registered cross-asset families under a
cumulative family-wise multiplicity correction, and nominated none. Both governed programs
produced valid null results. The legacy historical partitions (the aligned ETH/BTC daily
history through the research cutoff) are closed to further candidate-nomination research by the
permanent legacy-research moratorium. This plan does not reopen them.

### 16.2 What a future strategy-research phase REQUIRES (at least one)

A future phase that evaluates any new strategy candidate may begin only on a genuinely new
evidentiary basis. It requires at least one of the following, established and frozen before any
candidate is designed:

1. A genuinely-new, pre-registered prospective dataset that was not available during the design
   of any prior candidate family.
2. A matured, independently-governed prospective cohort that has grown to a pre-agreed size
   under its own append-only governance, independent of the strategy researchers.
3. A materially different market or domain, carrying its own separately-frozen partition and its
   own stated economic hypothesis (not a re-labelling of the ETH/BTC history).
4. External, independent research supplied by a party with no access to the sealed results,
   whose work can be reviewed without exposing any sealed partition.

Any one of these is necessary but not sufficient. The phase still needs a fresh pre-registration,
a fresh multiplicity-budget accounting that does not reset the prior families, and a new human
authorization (see 16.5).

### 16.3 What is REJECTED outright

The following are not new evidence. Each would convert disciplined research into strategy mining
on a closed partition, and each is refused before any calculation, engine call, registry append,
or result publication:

- Another candidate sweep on the closed ETH/BTC history.
- Tiny parameter variations of an already-evaluated family.
- Another trend wrapper.
- Another moving-average combination.
- Another Donchian alias.
- Another volatility overlay.
- Using the development gate as a discovery surface.
- Opening the final holdout out of curiosity.
- Activating any strategy that carries no nomination.

None of these is made legitimate by a new package version, a changed benchmark, or a
re-combination of existing signals. Freshness cannot be manufactured from the closed history.

### 16.4 A future DATA-ONLY prospective program (described, NOT activated)

One legitimate way to build the genuinely-new basis of 16.2(2) is a data-only prospective
program. It is described here for planning only and is explicitly NOT activated, scheduled, or
wired to run by this document:

- Scope: daily ETH/BTC cohort growth. Each day appends newly-closed, fully-formed daily candles
  to an append-only raw-data store.
- Append-only raw data: the store is never rewritten; corrections are additive records, and
  every appended body carries its own acquisition provenance.
- Human-reviewed proposals: each append arrives as a human-reviewed proposal, never an automated
  write.
- No strategy calculation: the program computes no strategy and evaluates no candidate.
- No signal generation: it produces no trading signal, position, or target of any kind.
- No performance metric: it computes no return, Sharpe, drawdown, or any performance statistic.
- No auto-merge: nothing merges automatically; every change waits for explicit human review.
- No sealed access: it reads no development gate, no final holdout, and no prospective ledger.
- Separate future authorization: the program itself, and any later use of the cohort it grows
  for strategy research, each require their own separate future human authorization.

The program only grows and governs data. Turning any grown cohort into strategy research is a
distinct, later, separately-authorized step that must itself satisfy 16.2 and 16.5. Documenting
this program is not a decision to run it.

### 16.5 The next phase is NOT automatically V2C

Completion of the V2A-V2B stacked acceptance does not start V2C, and does not start any next
phase. There is no automatic successor. After stacked acceptance the branches stay open and
draft, the sealed partitions stay sealed, no tag or release is cut, and no strategy work
resumes. Any next phase, whether it is called V2C or anything else, requires a new and explicit
human decision taken after the stacked acceptance is recorded. This document neither makes nor
pre-approves that decision.
