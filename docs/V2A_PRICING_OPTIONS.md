# V2A Commercial / Pricing Options — PRIVATE DRAFT

> **Status: PRIVATE INTERNAL DRAFT. Not an offer. Not a price list. Not a commitment.**
> This document explores *hypothetical* commercial structures for internal planning only. The
> program's standing posture is `not_sell_ready`; nothing here is offered for sale, and no price,
> term, or structure here is authorized, agreed, or extended to any party. Any actual commercial
> arrangement requires separate human authorization, legal review, and a signed agreement.

## Purpose

To think through *how* a research-stage ETH/USD research program **could** be evaluated by a
prospective buyer without disclosing source, sealed data, or any live capability — and what
commercial shapes an eventual arrangement might take **if** the program ever became sell-ready. It
does not assert that the program is ready, valuable, or for sale.

## What is (and is not) on the table today

- **On the table (evaluation only):** a source-free, redacted diligence bundle — the evaluation
  contract, the claims catalogue, the factsheet, and the commercial-readiness scorecard — served
  through the reference evaluation gateway. See `src/eth_research/buyer/`.
- **Never on the table here:** source code, the sealed development-gate / final-holdout partitions,
  raw or canonical price data, credentials, live or paper exchange access, or the ability to run the
  governed experiment. These are enumerated as *withheld* in the evaluation contract.

## Illustrative commercial structures (hypothetical)

These are sketches of *shapes*, deliberately without numbers, because no price is authorized.

1. **Evaluation license (time-boxed, source-free).** A prospective buyer receives the redacted
   diligence bundle under NDA for a fixed evaluation window, with no rights beyond evaluation. See
   `docs/V2A_EVAL_LICENSE_DRAFT.md`.
2. **Independent development-gate review engagement.** A separately-governed, independently-run
   evaluation of a nominated candidate on a sealed partition — explicitly **out of scope for V2A**
   and requiring separate authorization. V2A only *nominates eligibility* for such a review.
3. **Research collaboration.** Joint work under a collaboration agreement with shared governance;
   no transfer of source or sealed data implied.
4. **Asset transfer (far future, gated).** Only conceivable after out-of-sample / forward evidence
   exists, a license is chosen by a human, and IP readiness is established. Not applicable now.

## Pricing philosophy (for a hypothetical future, not today)

- Price would track *demonstrated, independently-verified* evidence, not research-train results.
- Research-train performance is **not** a basis for any price; it is not out-of-sample.
- Any figure would be set by a human with legal and commercial advice, never inferred by this system.

## Hard gates before any commercial step

- A human explicitly authorizes a commercial process.
- A license is chosen (none is present in this repository; that is a deliberate human gate).
- Independent out-of-sample / forward evidence exists (does not exist within V2A).
- Legal review of NDA, evaluation license, and IP position is complete.

Until every gate is cleared, the answer to "what is the price?" is: **there is none — it is not for
sale.**
